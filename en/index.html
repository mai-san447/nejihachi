The Nejihachi picture book series

A little honeybee made of screwsNejihachi

For little ones who get flustered when things go wrong.
For grown-ups who keep saying "Hurry up!"

Bugs made of screws and tools set off to fix the troubles of their forest. Short, simple sentences make these books easy to read aloud with children ages 3 to 7.

"Don't rush. Stop and look closely."

Book 1

Nejihachi and the Valley of the Wind

The wind has vanished from the forest of gears. The windmills stand still, and not a single leaf moves. So Nejihachi sets off for the Valley of the Wind. On the way, he meets Denko, a calm stag beetle made of wires, whose eyes light up dark paths.

In the middle of the valley, a giant fan is pulling in all the wind. Pushing with all his might only makes things worse. What will Nejihachi do? Every time he turns a screw, it goes "Twist, twist, click!"

For grown-ups

A story about slowing down

Nejihachi fails when he uses force, and succeeds when he turns the screw slowly. When your child gets flustered, try saying "Twist, twist, click!" out loud together.

Look for screws with a grown-up

After the mini guide, some children want to find real screws. Small parts can be swallowed by accident, so please stay close and keep them out of reach afterward.
